ℹ️ Understanding the Metrics

Total Videos All videos found for this topic in the last 28 days
Recent (7 Days) Videos published in the past week (shows recent momentum)
Outlier Videos (>20K) 👁️ Clickable: Shows all videos with >20K views. Count displayed smartly (20+, 40+, etc.)
Unique Creators Number of different channels covering this topic (shows breadth)
🚀 Trend Velocity Indicates if trend is accelerating, stable, or dying based on age distribution & performance
Momentum Score (0-100) 50% Frequency + 50% Viral. Frequency = video volume/recency. Viral = high-view videos + creator diversity + engagement.
